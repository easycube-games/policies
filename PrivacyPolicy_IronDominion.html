Privacy Policy — Iron Dominion

Effective date: September 8, 2026

Easy Cube Games ("we", "us", or "our") provides Iron Dominion, an Android game with package name com.easycube.irondominion. This policy explains what information is processed when you use the game, why it is used, who receives it, and your choices.

Information Stored on Your Device

Iron Dominion does not require a player account or ask for your name, email address, or phone number to play. Game progress and preferences are stored locally on your device. These include your champions, upgrades, in-game resources, chests, squad, difficulty, run progress, tutorial progress, language, sound settings, and gameplay statistics.

We do not provide an account-based cloud save service. Android or your device provider may back up app data according to your device's backup settings. Such backups are managed by that provider.

Firebase Analytics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is used and improve the player experience. It processes app usage information, such as app launches, sessions, engagement, and events recorded by the game, together with app version, device model, operating system, language, and a randomly generated app-instance identifier.

Analytics can derive an approximate location, such as a country or region, from your device's IP address. This does not require access to your device's precise GPS location. Depending on the Android version, device privacy settings, and Analytics configuration, Analytics may also process the Android advertising identifier. These identifiers are not your name or email address, but they can distinguish an app installation or device and should not be treated as fully anonymous.

Firebase Crashlytics

We use Firebase Crashlytics to detect, investigate, and fix crashes and other errors. Reports may include error messages and stack traces, timestamps, app version, operating system, device model, memory and storage information, app state, and installation or session identifiers generated by Firebase.

To help reproduce an error, reports can also contain limited gameplay context: the map floor and node type, battle identifier and outcome, run phase, selected difficulty, in-game gold, current screen, and progress statistics such as runs started, wins, and tutorial stage. These reports support troubleshooting; they are not a cloud backup of your save.

Information You Send to Us

If you contact us for support or about privacy, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include, such as a description of a problem or an attachment. We use this information to respond and handle your request. Please do not send passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information that is not needed for your request.

How Information Is Used and Shared

We use the information described above to operate and improve Iron Dominion, understand usage, diagnose technical problems, respond to support requests, and meet applicable legal obligations. We do not sell your personal information.

Google receives analytics and diagnostic data through its Firebase and Google Analytics services. Google processes that information under the terms and privacy rules applicable to those services. We may also disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the security of the service or the rights of our users.

You can read more about Google's handling of information here:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Privacy and Security in Firebase
  • Google Analytics privacy and data safeguards

Advertising, Purchases, and Cookies

Iron Dominion contains no third-party advertising and no real-money purchases. Its resources and rewards are part of gameplay. The game does not use browser cookies, but Firebase and Analytics use the app and device identifiers described above.

Data Retention

Local game data remains on your device until it is erased or replaced. Firebase Crashlytics retains crash traces and associated identifiers for 90 days before starting removal from its live and backup systems, as described in Firebase's retention information.

Analytics user and event data is retained according to the settings of our Google Analytics property and Google's service rules. Retention controls and their limits, including the separate treatment of aggregated reports, are described in Google Analytics data retention. We retain support correspondence only for as long as needed to handle the request and any applicable legal obligations.

Your Choices and Data Deletion

You can erase your local game progress using Settings → Erase progress and confirming the action. You can also clear the app's storage through Android settings or uninstall the app to remove data from the current installation. Device backups, if any, are controlled through your device provider's settings.

Erasing local progress or uninstalling the game does not automatically delete analytics or crash reports already sent to Google. To request access to or deletion of information we hold about you, contact us at the address below. Because there are no player accounts, your email address alone may not identify a particular analytics or crash record. We may ask for information reasonably needed to verify and locate the relevant records.

You can manage the Android advertising identifier through your device's privacy settings, where available. This does not disable all analytics or crash reporting. Depending on applicable law, you may also have rights to correct your information, object to or restrict processing, withdraw consent where processing relies on consent, or raise a concern with your local data protection authority. Contact us to exercise these rights.

Security and International Processing

Firebase and Google Analytics encrypt data in transit. We limit access to information to what is needed for the purposes described in this policy. No method of transmission or storage can guarantee complete security.

Google may process information in countries other than your own, including the United States. Its applicable data processing terms and safeguards are explained in the Google and Firebase privacy information linked above.

Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect children's personal information in violation of applicable law. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided personal information to us, please contact us. We will review the situation and take appropriate steps, including deletion where required.
